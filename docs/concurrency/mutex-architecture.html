Mutex architecture — who touches what

Zero locks left. Every sync.Mutex/Cond in non-test Go has been deleted, each by giving its state one owner rather than by narrowing the lock. Each diagram below is now historical record — the real goroutines, the real methods, the real line numbers, and what replaced the lock.

  • gone · PacedWire.mu — deleted by making the wire a goroutine, not a smaller lock
  • gone · outbox.mu + cond — deleted by per-direction channels, not a smaller lock
  • gone · Trace.mu — deleted by giving the drain the only write, not by a smaller lock
  • gone · LayoutHolder.mu — deleted by ownership, not by a smaller lock
  • gone · scene_persist — the second party was a timer goroutine
  • gone · RealClock.mu — deleted by ownership, not by a smaller lock
Verified against the tree on task/framing-event-order. Line numbers drift — re-grep before trusting them. Companion to docs/concurrency/framings.md, which records why each lock exists; this page records who touches it.
