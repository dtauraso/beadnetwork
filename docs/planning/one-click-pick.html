One-click pick

Proposal for the step list. Today a link has to be picked before any of its numbers can change (Go refuses the edit otherwise), and picking and opening are two separate targets on the same row. This page proposes making the row a single target.

Today vs. proposed

What one click does

Moving the pick still shows the existing “moved the pick” toast with Undo.

Unpicking (rare, so it gets the small target)

Quick look (rare, so it only shows on hover)

Looking at an unpicked link is the least common thing done on a row, so look shows only while the pointer is over an unpicked row, in place of “not turned”, which says nothing for an unpicked link anyway. It opens the inspector with the pick unchanged; the steppers there stay dimmed and the header switch picks from there. A picked row never shows it.

No near misses. The whole right 30% of the hovered row is look, its edge marked by a hairline. A 12px strip before it does nothing, so a click that lands just short of look is dropped instead of picking the link. The dashed strip under the mock shows the click zones; it is not drawn in the editor.
