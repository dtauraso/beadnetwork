One-click pick

Proposal for the step list. Today a link has to be picked before any of its numbers can change (Go refuses the edit otherwise), and picking and opening are two separate targets on the same row. This page proposes making the row a single target.

What it takes today vs. proposed

What one click does

Moving the pick still shows the existing “moved the pick” toast with Undo, so a stray click costs one more click to take back.

Unpicking (rare, so it gets the small target)

Risk

  • Opening an unpicked link just to read its numbers would now pick it. Hovering a row already lights its edge in the scene, and an unpicked link has no turn of its own to read, so little is lost. Undo on the toast covers the rest.
  • START rows stay as they are: they are not picked per step, so their click only opens.

Change, if agreed

  • Builder: that act sends the existing pick edit when the link is not picked, then opens its card, the same as the row click does now.
