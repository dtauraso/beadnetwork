Steps

Each step is a column you open on its own

Steps sit left to right in sequence order. Each opens and closes independently, so you can keep the two steps you are comparing open and everything else out of the way.

Step tools

An open step's header has a second line of tools. These replace F2, the old drag-to-reorder and the step bar.
