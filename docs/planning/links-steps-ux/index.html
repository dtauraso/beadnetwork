Proposal

Many steps, many nodes, many links open at once

The score-plus-inspector layout let you edit one link at a time. This layout is built for the way the tab was actually used: open several steps, open several nodes inside each, open several links' cards and edit them side by side, and press every picked link in a step from its HEAD row. Everything is reachable with a click, a drag or a two-finger scroll.

The four things it is built for

1 · Several steps open

Each step is a column with its own ▾/▸. Open ones sit side by side; a closed one shrinks to a thin strip with its number. Two-finger scroll moves sideways.

2 · Several nodes open in a step

Inside an open step, each node is a row that folds on its own. Folding node 1 in step 1 leaves node 1 in step 2 as it was.

3 · Several links open

Clicking a link row opens its card in place, under its node, in its step. Any number stay open, across nodes and steps, each with dials and steppers.

4 · HEAD in every open step

The top row of each open step: φ, θ, r locks, each with ▲▼ that press every picked link in that step at once.
