3D Camera Navigation

branch: task/camera-nav-doc  ·  tools/topology-vscode/src/webview/three/ — editor camera controls
Controls Camera model Motions Fit & persistence Design

Three independent camera motions, each with its own input and math:

Arcball rotate — camera arcs around the persistent target.
ROT_SPEED = 0.005 rad/px
Pan — camera and target slide together on the camera right/up basis.
Scale = distance to target (no z=0).
Dolly — camera moves toward the target.
offset = (camPos − target)·1.01^deltaY, clamped to a min distance (never crosses the target).
