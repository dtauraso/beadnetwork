The score

Like a music score or a tracker: time runs left to right, one row per voice. Here a voice is a link and time is the step list. You read a whole sequence at a glance and compare steps by looking sideways.

Rows: grouped by the node they point into

Every edge in the 3D scene is the same colour, so a row is matched to its edge by hovering: the hovered row thickens its edge. The pick rule lives on the group: one ● per group per column, which the layout makes obvious before any rule is broken.

Each header names the step (F2 renames) and shows its HEAD locks as three labeled chips: · free, ↑ hold up, ↓ hold down. Today's unlabeled glyphs get their axis letter.

Clicking a header scrubs the scene to that step (the existing ShowStep); the underline marks the one shown. A step holding steps shows as ▸ approach and expands in place into grouped sub-columns.

Drag a header to reorder (MoveStep). The trailing ＋ appends a step; ⌘D duplicates the focused one.
