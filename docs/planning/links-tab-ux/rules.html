Each rule, before and after

Today a rule shows up as a red sentence after you broke it (the "before" messages are paraphrased). Each rule below gets a control shape that can't express the broken state, or explains itself before you try.

1 · At most one picked link into a node

Before
3 → 1 pick

After
into 1 · step 1
2 → 1
3 → 1
Moved the pick from 2→1 to 3→1 Undo ⌘Z

Picks inside a group act like radio buttons. Picking 3→1 un-picks 2→1 and says so, with undo.
