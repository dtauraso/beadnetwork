Links tab — proposed layout

A mock-up to look at, not the editor. A dropdown picks the sequence, and buttons on the line below pick the step; each node is one line; each link is a card with φ, θ, r as rows and at, Δ, nΔ, 1 as columns. Click a sequence, a step, a node name or a link name to see it react.

What changed from the tab today

  • The nine stacked sequence buttons become one dropdown; + and − beside it replace “new sequence” and “delete sequence”.
  • Steps 1 and 2 no longer stack; the step buttons pick which one shows.
  • A node’s s, its reference Δφ/Δθ, the φ+θ+r lock and “link to” share the node’s line, so a folded node is one line and the “ref 1” box is gone.
  • Each link is a card 4 columns wide instead of a 13-column row, so θ’s 1 column and all of r are no longer off the edge, and cards sit side by side.
  • Arrows sit on the column heads (Δ⬍ steps that column for this link) and on the axis labels (φ⬍ steps φ’s Δ), not on every number; a number is typed by clicking it.
  • A picked link is one thick border around its card instead of outlines on its name, Δ and nΔ cells.
  • A refused press shows its message under the card it came from.
