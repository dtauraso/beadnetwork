What separation is

separation is how far apart two states are on the ring, the SHORT way round — never more than a half turn. It is not a resting-state test: it names no halt of its own, and it is the ONLY thing the perpendicular and parallel modes share. Everything else about each mode — what counts as its halt, and which way is closer to it — is read straight off this one number, against the counts that mode stops at.
