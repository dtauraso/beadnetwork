Four drawn vectors — three from this node's index, one from its channel

top tilt (stored) bottom (+12) coplanar normal (+6) — and what is sent received — the PARTNER's coplanar normal
Three of the four are this index's own arithmetic and move together when t steps. The fourth is not derived here at all: it is whatever last arrived, held until the next arrival replaces it, and it is the only one a reset can remove on its own.
The received arrow is the partner's blue arrow, unrotated — the two are the same direction, drawn at two centres. In the editor these are the pink trio and the cyan single arrow; the colours here match.

What the partner does with it

arrival v against both nodes of a pair — same unmodified rule
d(v, top) ∈ {0…5, 19…23} t ← t + 1
else d(v, bottom) ∈ {0…5, 19…23} t ← t − 1
d ∈ {6, 18} against both no step · no send · no bead — the halt
The halt does not depend on which way round a direction is stated: 6 and 18 are the two separations a half turn maps onto themselves, so a direction and its opposite are perpendicular to the same things. The step signs run the same, unmodified, on both nodes of a pair.
