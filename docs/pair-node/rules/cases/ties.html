There is no tie to break

The rule asks two questions in order — is the TOP inside a quarter turn of the arrival, then is the BOTTOM (offset) — and the two can never both be true: the ends are a half turn apart, so an arrival inside a quarter of one is outside a quarter of the other. Checked over every arrival on 12-, 24- and 48-point rings: both inside, 0 cases. So no ordering convention is doing any work, and the earlier tie-breaking ≤ is gone with the count it compared.
What remains is the third branch: NEITHER end inside a quarter turn, which is the halt rather than a tie — 108 of the 576 arrivals on a 24-point ring.
