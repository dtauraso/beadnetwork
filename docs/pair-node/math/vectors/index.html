Four drawn vectors — three from this node's index, one from its channel

top tilt (stored) bottom (+12) coplanar normal (+6) — and what is sent received — the PARTNER's coplanar normal
Three of the four are links off this node's own Top state and move together when Top steps. The fourth is not derived here at all: it is whatever last arrived, held until the next arrival replaces it, and it is the only one a reset can remove on its own.
The received arrow is the partner's blue arrow, unrotated — the two are the same direction, drawn at two centres. In the editor these are the pink trio and the cyan single arrow; the colours here match.

arrival IS this machine's halt stand still — reply anyway, so the partner has something to correct against
otherwise Top ← Top.next or Top.prev, whichever lands closer to this machine's own halt
in the SETTING mode (before START, or after RESET) an arrival moves nothing — every separation is that mode's home, so the node is already halted wherever it stands. The choice is made at START, not here
There is ONE machine with three modes (Cases), and a node is in exactly one of them. A chosen mode steps straight over the OTHER mode's halt on the way back to its own — see stepFromVector. The step itself is answerable everywhere on the ring, including sitting exactly on a halt.
