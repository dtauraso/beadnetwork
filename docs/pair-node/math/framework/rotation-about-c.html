The a→b vector on its sphere about node a

Node a is at (1, 0, 0); its vector to b has r = 1. One slider per angle of a→b; each changes only its own angle. b = Ca + polar_to_cart(r, φ, θ).

Drag the picture to look round the sphere.

Calculation, in order

    1. φ is measured from +y. Its slider tips a→b over the top and bottom of a; θ and r stay. Over its range b visits every point of the circle through the poles at the current θ.
    2. θ is measured round the vertical from +x. Its slider swings a→b round the vertical through a; φ and r stay. Over its range b visits every point of the circle at the current φ.
    3. The two are separate: each changes only its own angle, so order does not matter. Together they reach every point on the sphere.
    4. From O, b is read as cart_to_polar(b − O). Those angles are not φ and θ, which are about a.
