A spiral of spheres

Each sphere can turn round another sphere, at the distance it already has from it, and stops where it would touch any sphere instead of passing through it.
Pair tabs Three-node card a→b vectors a→b builder Sphere spiral
Click a sphere to make it the centre (orange ring); click it again to clear it. Drag any other sphere to turn it round the centre; with no centre picked, it turns round its neighbour toward the middle of the spiral. Drag empty space to look round; pinch or ctrl-scroll to zoom.
